# Design and narrative direction

## Player promise
A ridiculous little job today, an unforgettable larger adventure behind it. Short sessions must produce a discovery, a decision, or a tangible improvement. No mandatory login streaks or repetition to keep up.

## Premise (III prototype)
Grimwald's inhabitants can choose their lives after the imposed labels were removed. Former bosses want pensions, heroes charge appearance fees, and villages miss the income from visiting adventurers. #404 opens The Unfinished Business Bureau to solve unwanted problems and pay the rent. Seemingly disconnected crises hide someone manufacturing demand for a return to compulsory roles.

Working antagonist seed: an unnamed crisis broker benefits from fear and dependency. Reveal only forged orders and a recurring seal in the slice. Do not resurrect the Records or name the broker as the Archivist without a later story decision. Freedom can be difficult without being presented as a mistake.

Theme: what do we owe one another when nobody is assigned a purpose? Comedy targets institutions, vanity and unreasonable systems. Emotional scenes are allowed space; do not undercut every sincere moment with a joke.

## Initial party

## World structure
Bureau → Dregswell village → Mill Yard → Ledger Cellar. Dregswell and its NPC names are new prototype additions. This is one connected district in Grimwald, not an invented replacement for the established five regions. Leave its final regional placement unspecified.

Explore with visible encounter markers, conversation prompts and a small map. Completed jobs update NPC dialogue, environment details and services. Each job's branch requires only a few durable state flags, not a simulation of the entire town.

## Longer campaign direction (not implementation scope)
Five potential chapters: Bureau opening; growing regional cases; linked manufactured crises; towns deciding whether to surrender choice; confronting the crisis broker. This is an outline for later review. Do not generate the remaining chapters during the first coding pass.

## Content and progression rules
Three authored jobs form one causal chain, not three unrelated fetch quests. Optional repeatable salvage is openly labelled gameplay, not another supposedly unique story crisis. Main-job material rewards are guaranteed. No rare drop gates the main path. Choices lead to different dialogue, services and a persistent visual change, not an unimplemented promise.

Completion ends with a playable free-roam state and a clear “End of prototype” notice in the journal. Never imply the five-chapter campaign is already present.
